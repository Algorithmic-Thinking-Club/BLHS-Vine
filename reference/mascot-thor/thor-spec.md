# Thor — character bible (the main player sprite)

> Sourced from 3 real references: the official Panthers athletics logo (`blhs-panther-logo.png`),
> the costume/fursuit (Welcome Back Assembly 25-26, @blhspanthers), and the friendly cartoon mascot.
> Thor is the player character (anthropomorphic panther; player picks name + gender, base = Thor).
> Every generated Thor — walk sprite, portrait, combat pose — must match this. Not a generic cat.

## Identity
- **Thor**, the BLHS Panther. Black panther, athletic, friendly-heroic by default, fierce when competing.
- Two expression poles to support: **friendly/heroic** (cartoon: winking, big grin, thumbs up) for the
  avatar + most dialogue, and **fierce/snarling** (logo) for varsity/competition/boss moments.

## Form & markings
- Fur: **black / near-black charcoal**, with subtle cool (slightly blue/teal) sheen, never brown.
- Muzzle + inner face + inner ears: **light warm gray**; nose **black**; **white fangs**; whiskers.
- **White / silver eyebrow tufts** above the eyes (strong identifier from the costume).
- Hands/paws: dark gray-black; cartoon shows gloved-looking paws.
- Eyes: **bright TEAL / cyan** — the signature feature. Always teal, never yellow/green.

## Wardrobe & brand
- Wears a **teal team tee**: "BLHS" (full) or "BL" monogram wordmark in black with white outline.
- Brand colors: **teal + black + white/gray**. Teal is the accent everywhere (eyes, shirt, trim).

## Working palette (approx, refine by sampling the saved refs)
- fur black: #16171a · charcoal: #292b30 · muzzle gray: #8b8f95 · light gray: #c0c4c8
- teal (eyes/shirt/brand): #15b3c3 · deep teal: #138f9c · white: #f5f4ef · nose/black trim: #0c0d0f

## Game use
- **Top-down walk sprite** (4-direction, 16-bit) = the overworld avatar; **portrait bust** = dialogue/UI.
- Keep silhouette readable at small size: black body + teal eyes + teal shirt reads instantly as Thor.
- Player name/gender customizes flavor, not the base panther design.
