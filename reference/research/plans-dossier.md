# BLHS Plans Dossier — Architectural & Civil Engineering Sources

Research compiled 2026-06-24. Goal: find authoritative, to-scale architectural and civil
plans for Bonney Lake High School (Sumner-Bonney Lake SD, WA) to build the game's campus map.

All downloaded PDFs live in `reference/research/plan-pdfs/`; rendered pages in
`plan-pdfs/png/` (low-res scan) and `plan-pdfs/hires/` (300 DPI crops). These are GITIGNORED.

district's SEPA notices page (not posted as plan PDFs as of this research). Civil engineer of
record on the expansion = **Sitts & Hill Engineers**.

## What each plan shows (read off the actual drawings)

### A. Whole-campus SITE PLAN (VE report doc p.6 → png/value-engineering-008.png)
The authoritative to-scale campus layout. Reading the drawing:
- **Existing High School** = central angled building cluster (the radiating learning-community
  wings fan out east/northeast from a two-story commons).
- **ADDITION** = new wing attached on the south/front face of the existing building (between the
  existing school and the parking lot). This is the 3-story, ~44,000 SF expansion.
- **BUS LOOP** = far west (left) end of campus.
- **FIRE LANE** = runs along the north edge of the building.
- **EXISTING PARKING LOT** = large lot south of the building (center-bottom), with more parking
  to the south.
- Zone **L-102** (northeast): **MULTI-USE FIELD** (top) + **FASTPITCH/SOFTBALL FIELD** (diamond).
- Zone **L-103** (southeast): **BASEBALL FIELD** (diamond), reached by a new **ENTRY DRIVE**.
- **199TH AVE CT E** runs north-south on the far east edge; the new **multi-lane ROUNDABOUT**
  sits where the entry drive meets 199th Ave Ct E.
- Hi-res legible crop: `hires/ve-siteplan-crop.png`.

### B. FIRST FLOOR PLAN – OVERALL (VE report doc p.7 → value-engineering-009.png)
Entire campus footprint with every room. Geometry legible at 300 DPI (`hires/ve-firstfloor-crop.png`):
- Large angled gym/PAC volume top-left; central commons spine; classroom wings fanning to the
  east; new addition wing at the bottom/front. Individual room labels are small but present.

### C. Floor Addition enlargements (VE report, with KEY PLANS showing where the wing attaches)
- **First Floor – Addition** (doc p.8 → value-engineering-010.png): DLC classrooms, Life Skills
  Kitchen, Electrical, Mechanical, Storage, Student Engagement, Custodial, IDF, Office.
- **Second Floor – Addition** (doc p.10 → value-engineering-012.png).
- **Third Floor – Addition** (doc p.11 → value-engineering-013.png): classroom wing + key plan.

### D. Design Development floor plans (BCRA, March 2026) — cleanest area callouts
From `hires/dd-ground.png`, `dd-first.png`, `dd-second.png`:
- **Ground floor**: rooms incl. MECHANICAL (2,401 SF), DLC CLASSROOM (817 SF / 41 occ),
  LIFE SKILLS KITCHEN (510 SF), DLC CLASSROOM (819 SF), TEACHER WORKROOM, ELECTRICAL, STORAGE,
  OFFICE, IDF, FIRE RISER, CUSTODIAL; a "GRADE BELOW / SLAB ABOVE" note (the addition steps with
  the hillside — "Build the Hill"). Has a KEY PLAN.
- **First floor** (campus-wide footprint with zones):
  - AREA 500 = 4,557 SF "NO WORK"
  - AREA 100 = 41,319 SF (includes areas of renovation)
  - AREA 200 = 13,035 SF (includes areas of renovation)
  - ADDITION = 12,300 SF (new wing, lower-left)
  - RENOVATION zones = 1,728 SF and 1,317 SF
- **Second floor**: AREA 100 = 27,698 SF, AREA 200 = 9,624 SF, ADDITION = 16,760 SF,
  RENOVATION = 6,805 SF and 1,107 SF. The addition reads as a 3-story wing at lower-left.

### E. BCRA Schematic Design deck (Jan 2026) — program color-coding
Already in repo (`reference/floorplans-maps/rendered/`). Color legend by program:
ADMIN/TEACHER SUPPORT, CAREER CENTER, CIRCULATION, FLEX, SERVICES/RESTROOM, DLC, JROTC,
LEADERSHIP, SCIENCE, MEETING, SERVICED CLASSROOM, WELCOME CENTER, STORAGE, GEN-ED CLASSROOMS.
Third-floor results: 12 general classrooms, 3 science labs, 3 DLC classrooms = 18 added learning
spaces. Site-plan goals: improve circulation, safe pedestrian paths, increased queuing, updated
drop-off, DLC entry with bus drop-off near classrooms.

## Authoritative campus facts (cross-checked, with sources)

- Campus = **49 acres**; **207,539 total building SF**; **opened 2005**; original project
  **$14.9M**. (EMA via district: https://www.sumnersd.org/about-us/overview/construction/projects/bonney-lake-high-school-improvements)
- Original EMA design = **small learning communities radiating from a two-story commons**; each
  community = **5 classrooms around a central project area** + group-work office + shared faculty
  office; lecture halls with theater seating (later converted to classrooms).
  EMA also lists **186,320 SF, phased 2006 & 2011**, initial 1,396 → 1,400 students.
  (https://www.ericksonmcgovern.com/bonney-lake-hs)
- **PAC** = 15,523 SF, 1 story, 550 seats, **completed Fall 2019**, full fly loft, adjustable
  acoustics; "centerpiece adjacent to the existing building."
  (https://www.ericksonmcgovern.com/pac-bonney-lake)
- **2024 bond expansion (BCRA):** ~44,000 SF new + 16,000 SF renovation; **3 stories**;
  **18 added learning spaces** (12 gen classrooms, 3 science labs, 3 DLC); expanded CTE,
  Welcome Center, consolidated student services/counseling; new **DLC entry + bus drop-off**.
  Athletic: baseball, fastpitch/softball, and multi-use fields converted to **synthetic turf +
  lighting**. Traffic: improved queuing/drop-off + **multi-lane roundabout at 199th Ave Ct E**.
  Budget **$76.9M** (district) / $76.4M grand total (GC/CM deck). Construction 2026-2028,
  substantial completion ~**Fall 2028**.
- **Project team (expansion):** Architect **BCRA, Inc.**; GC **Korsmo Construction**; Civil
  **Sitts & Hill Engineers**. (district bond page)
- **Improvements/stadium team (earlier):** EMA (arch), BCE Engineers (MEP), PCS Structural
  (structural), **AHBL (civil for stadium)**, AllPlay Systems; covered grandstand + press box added.

## Uncertainty / gaps (do not invent)

- **No published full civil set** (grading, paving, utilities, landscape construction sheets).
  The VE site plan is the best to-scale site document found. To get the real civil set, request
  the City of Bonney Lake civil permit file (Sitts & Hill Engineers) or the district's full DD/CD
  drawing package.
- Original **2005 floor plans** (Erickson McGovern) were not found as a downloadable file; the
  current footprint is fully captured by the VE "First Floor Plan – Overall," which shows the
  existing building (the bulk of the campus) plus the new addition.
- Square-footage figures vary by source (186,320 SF design vs. 207,539 SF total building) — likely
  net-vs-gross or pre/post-addition; treat the larger as total gross building area.
- BCRA's own bcradesign.com project page for BLHS was not retrieved (firm site not indexed in the
  searches); renderings are available via the district pages instead.
