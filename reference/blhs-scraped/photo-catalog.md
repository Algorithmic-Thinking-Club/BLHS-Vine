# BLHS scraped photo catalog

> Real, public photos of Bonney Lake High School (10920 199th Ave Ct E, Bonney Lake, WA 98391;
> Sumner-Bonney Lake SD; Panthers; opened Sept 2005), harvested 2026-06-25 for 1:1 campus reference.
> Reference research only. Every file here is third-party copyrighted material held LOCALLY for art
> direction. Never commit, never redistribute. Folder is under the gitignored `reference/` tree.
>
> 59 images downloaded. All checked: zero duplicates inside this folder, and the 7 Erickson McGovern
> main-building shots that also appear here were REMOVED because they are byte-identical to files
> already in `reference/campus-exterior/ema-*` and `reference/campus-interior/ema-*`. The 27 PAC
> shots below are distinct higher-res variants, NOT dupes of the older `reference/campus-*/pac-*`.
>
> FERPA note: a few frames contain identifiable students (flagged per row). They are kept only for
> the architecture/palette behind the people. Do not surface student faces in any built asset.

## Coverage vs. the goal areas
- Main entrance / drive-loop / office: GAP for a real current photo. Covered by RENDERINGS of the new
  Welcome Center entry + the older EMA canopy-entrance photo already in `campus-exterior/`. The real
  current front entrance is best grabbed by a human from Google Street View / FB / IG (see leads).
- Courtyards + patios: real EMA courtyard photos already live in `campus-exterior/` (ema-courtyard,
  Bonney+Lake+HS+01). No new ones here; band-Flickr could yield more (human screen for faces).
- Band room / music-wing courtyard: GAP. Best lead = the BLHS Band Flickr (43k photos, human-mine).
- Stairs + retaining walls + gates: `blhs-stairs-construction.jpg` (real exterior stairs/retaining
  wall mid-build) + the EMA `BLHS+stairs.JPG` already in `campus-interior/ema-stairs`.
- PAC: HEAVILY covered now (27 distinct pro shots: exterior all sides, lobby, auditorium, stage,
  rigging, seating angles) + 1 real hi-res exterior + 3 renderings.
- Commons: `blhs-commons-openhouse-event.jpg`, `blhs-interior-flexspace-commons.jpg` (real interiors).
- Gym: GAP (no public interior photo auto-downloadable; athletics gallery + FIRST WA are bot-blocked).
- Athletic fields / track / stadium: `blhs-stadium-grandstand`, `-stands`, `-dusk-cheer-team` (real).
- Parking lots: `blhs-portables-parkinglot.jpg` (real, shows a lot + portables).
- Signage / monument: GAP for a real monument photo. The "BLHS" vertical sign appears on the
  Welcome-Center rendering; brand wordmark in `blhs-brand-panther-wordmark.webp`.
- Interiors (classrooms / halls / labs): real shots of a CTE/makerspace lab, learning-community
  flex space, lecture hall, hallway flex space, safety vestibule.
- Aerial / roof: GAP (Latitude aerial is paywalled/bot-blocked; repo already has aerial-esri.png).

   scrape) can page through. SCREEN FOR STUDENT FACES; pull the architecture/room frames. The 7
